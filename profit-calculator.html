What is a Profit Calculator?

A profit calculator helps shopkeepers and small business owners calculate how much profit or loss they make when selling a product.

How to Calculate Profit?

Profit is calculated by comparing the selling price with the purchase price. If the selling price is higher than the purchase price, you make a profit. If it is lower, you make a loss.

Who Can Use This Calculator?

This calculator is useful for shopkeepers, retailers, resellers, small businesses, students and anyone who wants to calculate profit quickly.
