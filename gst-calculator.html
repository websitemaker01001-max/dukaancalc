What is GST?

GST stands for Goods and Services Tax. It is an indirect tax charged on the supply of many goods and services in India.

How does this calculator work?

Enter the original amount and GST percentage. The calculator shows the GST amount and the final amount including GST.

Example

For ₹1,000 at 18% GST, the GST amount is ₹180 and the total amount is ₹1,180.
